What this number actually measures

This counts topics whose previous-year questions you have solved — not hours studied, and not how well a topic is known. Reading a chapter or writing a note leaves a topic at zero on this page. So treat it as a floor on your progress, not a ceiling: the real number is at least this, never less. It updates the moment you mark a topic done in assets/pyq.js.
